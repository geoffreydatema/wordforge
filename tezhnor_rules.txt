If a word contains a sound produced by one of the complex consonants, that single consonant must be used.
    - "тc" must be "ц"
    - "cт" must be "პ"
    - "кc" must be "ს"
    - "cк" must be "պ"
    - "кp" must be "ը"
    - "кв" must be "է"
    - "cв" must be "თ"
    - "zв" must be "რ"
    - "дв" must be "ც"

Double letters don't add any sound or phonetic modification to the word, and may not be used.

Vowels may not be combined. Many vowel combinations are too awkward to pronounce (such as "ջэ") but others such as "иэ" must be spelled "иьэ" to clearly distinguish each syllable.

Single vowel syllables are allowed only at the start of a word. Because vowel combinations are not allowed mid word, every other syllable is multi character.
